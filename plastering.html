Plastering

Walls and ceilings plastered flat, smooth and ready for any finish. We use professional-grade gypsum and cement plasters on residential and commercial projects across South Florida.

Start Your Project

Smooth surfaces, done right

Plaster is a craft. You either do it flat or you don't. We've plastered hundreds of rooms — apartments, offices, retail fit-outs, high-end condos.

The standard is always the same: straight lines, no bumps, clean corners. All our plasterers work by hand, checked with straightedges and spirit levels.
